What is Digital MapBoost?

Digital MapBoost is an SEO and digital growth agency in Sukarwadi, Borivali East, Mumbai, founded by Sanjiv Singh. It offers Local SEO, Google Business Profile optimization, website SEO, technical SEO, keyword research, social media optimization, Google Ads and Meta Ads.

What is Local SEO?

Local SEO helps a business appear for searches with local intent, such as "dentist near me" or "salon in Borivali", including Google Maps results.

Can you guarantee top rankings on Google?

No. Google decides rankings and nobody can honestly guarantee a position. A complete, accurate profile and helpful pages improve your chances and help customers trust you.

How long does SEO take to show progress?

It depends on competition, website condition and consistency. Small improvements may show in weeks, while stronger results usually take a few months.

Do you work with businesses outside Borivali?

Yes. Local SEO is done online, so businesses across Mumbai, including Kandivali, Dahisar and the western suburbs, can be supported.

Do I need a website for Local SEO?

A Google Business Profile can work without one, but a clear website gives far better long-term results.

Can you help my business appear in AI search tools like ChatGPT?

We can improve the signals these tools rely on: accurate business details, clear service pages, structured data and consistent profiles across the web. No one can guarantee a recommendation.
